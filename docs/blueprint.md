# **App Name**: Liberdade Financeira

## Core Features:

- Hero Section: Displays key sales information, including headline, subheadline, and video.
- Pain Points: Showcases the customer pain points to create a sense of empathy.
- Solution Presentation: Clearly presents the spreadsheet as the solution to financial disorganization.
- Bonus Highlights: Visually appealing cards detailing the bonuses offered with the premium plan.
- Social Proof Carousel: Displays customer testimonials and social proof.
- Pricing Options: Offer different plan options (Basic and Premium) in cards, highlighting the benefits and pricing for each. A limited-time urgency badge is included.
- FAQ Section: Displays frequently asked questions in an accordion style for easy readability.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant green (#24bc6e) for a fresh, energetic feel.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F0F0) to ensure content stands out.
- Accent color: Bright orange (#FFA500) to highlight important CTAs and badges, creating a sense of urgency.
- Headline font: 'Roboto Bold' for a modern, bold look. Body font: 'Open Sans' for readability.
- Use gradient backgrounds sparingly in header and CTA buttons to draw attention.
- Subtle animations for card entrances and carousel transitions to engage users.
- Use modern, flat icons that align with financial themes and vibrant color schemes.
